## Career discrepancy pattern

All 87 failures disagree at startup. A different, uniquely inferred seed restores
every checked observation in each diagnostic run. All 16 passing controls recover
their original seeds and reproduce their canonical inputs and outputs. Inferred
seeds are diagnostic evidence only and do not count as canonical passes.

A new comparison against the captured pre-race card and pinned goal route found:

- All 87 discrepancies occur where the original seed fails a mandatory Career goal.
- The official replay clears that goal in 59 cases and still fails it in 28.
- Another 38 mandatory-goal failures retain the original seed and match strictly.
- Of 726 strict matches, 199 are player losses; losing alone does not imply a mismatch.
- All 813 new Career captures use card 104101 and scenario 3. This limits the scope
  of conclusions drawn from this batch.

This supports investigating conditional goal-failure retries or result selection.
It does not establish a reroll probability, a replacement-seed rule, or capture
corruption. Treating the observed 87/125 fraction as a universal 70% mechanic would
be unsupported.

The association independently replicates in 100 older startup discrepancies:
all 100 original-seed results fail their mandatory goal. This sample contains
seven scenario 1 cases and 93 scenario 3 cases selected by replay hash. Official
replays clear 63 goals and still fail 37; one of 32 passing controls also fails
its goal without changing seed. These cases still all use card 104101, and the
sample was selected to investigate discrepancies, so it cannot estimate a reroll
probability. Historical retained outputs were inspected; no historical races
were re-simulated for this comparison.

Original start/end linkage is consistent across all 813 races. The inferred seeds
are absent from the checked response values and literal integer/text representations
in replay payloads. Every replay byte is accounted for, with no populated extension
sections or additional gzip metadata. Bounded random-stream shifts, reseeding and
simple seed transformations do not explain the failures. No applicable Career
retry rule was found in the pinned master data or extracted race parameters.

Further evidence should distinguish mandatory-goal failures that retain their seed
from those that receive a different replay. Preserve the full pre-race state, goal
route, every start/retry response and linked completion. An independently exposed
effective simulation seed or startup trace would be especially useful. Program 75,
particularly turn 66, is a high-yield target in this batch; it is not a general rule.
Testing failed mandatory goals for other cards would help establish how broadly
the association applies.
