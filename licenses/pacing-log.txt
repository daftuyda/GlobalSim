The experimental pacing logarithm's range reduction and split constants are
adapted from SunPro's single-precision log10f implementation, as published in:
https://github.com/bminor/glibc/blob/glibc-2.39/sysdeps/ieee754/flt-32/e_log10f.c

GlobalSim uses its existing double-to-single rounding for the inner natural
logarithm. This is not a general replacement for libc log10f.
